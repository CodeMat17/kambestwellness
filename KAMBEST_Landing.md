# KAMBEST TRADOMEDICAL SERVICES

## Landing-page strategy: Traditional Wellness. Informed Choices.

**Purpose:** Build a transparent, useful landing page that can be
assessed for Google Ads eligibility, encourages appropriate enquiries
through WhatsApp and telephone, and provides a clear route to the main
KAMBEST website.

**Important:** No landing-page wording, layout, subdomain, or redirect
can guarantee Google Ads approval. Google reviews the ad, the
landing-page experience, the destination and the services promoted. A
page must not be used to disguise the actual business, hide medicine
sales, or route ad reviewers and ordinary visitors to different
experiences. This strategy is designed for policy transparency---not to
bypass review.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Recommended strategy

Build a genuine informational page under the KAMBEST brand, with its own
useful content and clear contact details. It may link to the main
website, but it should not exist solely as a temporary doorway that
automatically redirects visitors to a product-sales homepage.

### Recommended visitor journey

1.  A person sees an advertisement for a specific, genuinely
    informational health topic.
2.  The advertisement opens the relevant informational landing page.
3.  The page provides useful, balanced information and clearly
    identifies KAMBEST as the publisher.
4.  The visitor can contact KAMBEST through WhatsApp or telephone for
    appropriate general enquiries.
5.  A clearly labelled link allows visitors to visit the main KAMBEST
    website for further company information.
6.  If the main website contains product listings, product claims,
    ordering or medicine-related services, those remain visible and
    accessible to Google's review. Do not conceal them or represent the
    business as information-only if it also markets products.

**Do not use automatic redirects, cloaking, device-based variations,
hidden links, or a separate "clean" page that sends Google reviewers and
ordinary visitors to different content.** A redirect does not remove the
policy implications of the final destination.

## 5. How to link to the main website safely

Use a normal, clearly labelled link rather than an automatic redirect.

**Recommended link text:**\
"Visit the main KAMBEST website for further information about the
organisation and its services."

Before publishing that link:

-   Review the destination homepage and every page a visitor can reach
    from it.
-   Ensure the destination is consistent with the advertisement and the
    informational page.
-   Do not hide product pages, prices, ordering facilities or
    medicine-related services from Google.
-   Do not send Google reviewers to one page and ordinary users to
    another.
-   Do not use an interstitial page whose only purpose is to pass ad
    traffic to a different destination.
-   Do not use URL redirects to evade healthcare, destination or
    misrepresentation policies.

If the main site remains a medicine/product sales website, seek
clarification on its eligibility before running ads to any page on the
same domain or to a page that links into that sales journey.

## 6. WhatsApp, privacy and measurement

The WhatsApp button should open the business's stated number using the
international-format link:

`https://wa.me/2348074442605`

The page should explain what enquiries the business can handle. Avoid
inviting visitors to submit sensitive medical details, diagnoses,
prescriptions or medical records through advertising forms or tracking
tools.

For measurement:

-   Measure page views and meaningful engagement.
-   A WhatsApp button click can be recorded as a click event.
-   A click does not prove that a WhatsApp conversation occurred or that
    an enquiry became a customer.
-   Do not send sensitive health information to Google Analytics, Google
    Ads or other advertising platforms.
-   Publish a clear privacy notice explaining what information is
    collected, why it is collected and how visitors can contact the
    business about privacy.

Use Google Tag Manager or Google tag only after configuring it
appropriately for privacy and consent requirements. Do not install
multiple competing tracking tags.

## 7. Google Ads preparation checklist

Complete these checks before creating or funding a campaign.

### Website checks

-   [ ] The landing page offers genuine, useful information.
-   [ ] The business identity and contact details are accurate.
-   [ ] The privacy notice is accessible.
-   [ ] Health content is accurate, balanced and appropriately sourced.
-   [ ] Claims are substantiated and not misleading.
-   [ ] The main website and linked pages have been reviewed.
-   [ ] There is no cloaking, deceptive redirect or hidden content.
-   [ ] The site works on mobile and uses HTTPS.
-   [ ] WhatsApp and telephone links work.

### Google checks

-   [ ] Read Google's current Healthcare and medicines policy.
-   [ ] Confirm whether the exact business and advertised service are
    eligible in Nigeria.
-   [ ] Confirm whether Google certification is required and whether it
    is available for the proposed activity and location.
-   [ ] Check the destination requirements and misrepresentation policy.
-   [ ] If uncertain, contact Google Ads support with the business
    model, target country and exact landing-page URL before spending
    money.
-   [ ] Do not assume that ad approval on one page approves the rest of
    the domain or all products.
